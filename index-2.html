Journalist & writer

I’m Yang, an independent journalist covering food and its broader connections to culture, history and politics.

My work has appeared in the South China Morning Post, Monocle, Cathay, The Japan Times, Documented and Sixth Tone.

After a decade in New York and a short stint in Hong Kong, I am now travelling through China in search of good food (stories).
